To install on iPhone: tap Share → Add to Home Screen. It then works offline.

How to play
  • Goal: pot all 15 balls, in any order, in as few shots as you can.
  • The break: you start with the cue ball in hand behind the line. Drag it where you like, then tap PLACE.
  • Aim: drag anywhere on the table. ⟲ ⟳ nudge the aim a hair at a time (hold to repeat).
  • Strength: drag the strength bar, then tap SHOOT. What you set is exactly what you get — no timing, no luck.
  • Spin (optional): tap the little cue ball to open the spin window and drag the pointer to where the tip should strike: high for follow, low for draw, left or right for side. Side spin changes how the ball comes off a cushion.
  • Guide: the ring is the ghost ball (where the cue ball touches). The object ball's line turns green when it's heading for a pocket; the blue line is where the cue ball goes after contact. Cut shots throw the object ball slightly, so allow a hair on thin cuts.
  • Fouls: potting the cue ball (scratch) or missing every ball adds a foul. After a scratch the cue ball is in hand anywhere. Your score is shots + fouls.
  • Pinch, scroll or 🔍 to zoom. Hold SHOOT while the balls run to fast-forward. 🎨 changes the cloth, rails and room.
  • Desktop: mouse to aim, ←/→ fine aim (Shift for bigger steps), ↑/↓ strength, Space or Enter to shoot/place, S spin window, Z zoom, M mute, N next track.
