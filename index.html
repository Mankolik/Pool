How to play
  • Goal: clear every rack in as few shots as you can. Each rack has a par, like a golf hole; nine racks make a tour.
  • Ball in hand: every rack starts with the cue ball in your hand — drag to place it (behind the head string for a break, anywhere on an open table), then tap PLACE.
  • Aim: drag anywhere on the table. ⟲ ⟳ fine-tune (hold to repeat). Each turn the cue starts lined up on the easiest pot it can see.
  • Guide: the ring is the ghost ball (where the cue ball will touch). The object ball's line turns green when it's heading for a pocket, red if that ball isn't legal to hit first. The blue line is where the cue ball goes after contact, bent by follow or draw.
  • Spin: drag the tip on the little cue ball or pick Follow, Draw, Stun or side. Follow rolls through, draw screws back, stun stops dead on a straight shot, side spin bends the bounce off a cushion.
  • Side spin isn't free: it pushes the cue ball slightly the other way (squirt), and the further from centre you hit, the bigger the risk of a miscue — shown under the picker.
  • Stroke: hold the button to draw the cue back, release to set the pace, then tap again as the marker crosses the white line. Early pushes the shot right, late pulls it left.
  • The pot mark on the meter is the slowest pace that still drops the ball you're aiming at. Go a touch firmer — but not much, or it'll rattle.
  • Throw: on cut shots the object ball is dragged a little towards the cue ball's path (and by side spin). The guide doesn't show it — allow for it on thin cuts.
  • Fouls (+1): scratching (cue ball in a pocket — then it's ball in hand anywhere), missing every ball, or hitting the wrong ball first in rotation racks.
  • Racks: Six-pack (any order), Open table (spread balls, any order), Rotation (lowest ball first), Nine-ball (lowest first; a legal 9 clears the rack early!) and Eight-ball (15 balls, the 8 last — sink it early for +2).
  • Venues: each seed plays in one — pub, tournament hall (fast cloth, tight pockets), casino (a lucky ★ pocket: −1 shot), saloon (dead cushions, and the table leans!), space station (low gravity) or beach bar (sand on the cloth slows the balls). 🎲 picks another.
  • Hold the button while the balls run to fast-forward. Pinch, scroll or 🔍 to zoom.
  • Menu (☰): scorecard, rack rules, sound effects, music, next track and the main menu.
  • Desktop: mouse to aim, click/space to shoot, ←/→ fine aim, W/S follow/draw, A/D side, X centre, Enter to place, Z zoom, C scorecard, M mute, N next track.
